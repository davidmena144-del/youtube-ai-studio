# Takka identity v2 (proposal, 22 Nov — waiting for the owner's approval)

Source of the brief: the owner sent six short-form videos by another creator as a reference and asked for
our own identity "so we are ahead". We take the principles, not the look.

## What the reference does (measured from the six clips)

- Canvas 9:16, light warm background with large soft blurred blobs; no texture.
- One accent colour (violet) on near-black text; white cards with a soft shadow; lots of empty space.
- Fixed header on every frame: three dots on one side, the handle on the other.
- Title above the card (two colours: black + accent word); small caption pill at the bottom, one or two
  words at a time, the newest word in the accent colour.
- One idea per frame; a new frame every 2–4 s; cards show a UI mock-up of the thing being described.
- Voice: one continuous take with no pause longer than 0.35 s, 40–135 s long, Gulf dialect.
- Hook patterns: bold claim ("X killed Y"), a direct prompt typed on screen, "how I made this".
- Ending: one call to action tied to a keyword comment.

## Ours (different on purpose)

- Background: cool off-white `#F4F6FA`, a faint dot grid, two soft blobs in the topic colour.
- Ink `#101426`; cards white with a 12 px ink outline and a hard 20 px offset shadow ("sticker" look, same
  line weight as the mascot). No blur shadows.
- One accent per topic: body/default `#FF3D4D`, animals `#FF9F1A`, nature `#12B76A`, tech `#2F6BFF`,
  space `#7A4DFF`. Highlight marker `#FFD84D`.
- Font: Kufam only. Title 900/120 px, caption 900/76 px, card text 700/54 px, details 40 px, hero number 300 px
  (on 1080×1920). Corner radius 72 px, safe margin 90 px.
- Header: segmented progress bar (one segment per fact), the Takka word-mark, the handle.
- Caption pill above the mascot, word-lit, key word in an accent box.
- Mascot: the filled character with a fixed red cap, in every video, reacting to each fact.
- Voice: Speko `inworld` / `Omar`, Egyptian, normal pace; captions follow the voice.
- Hooks: shocking number, myth-break, open question, direct command, quick promise.
